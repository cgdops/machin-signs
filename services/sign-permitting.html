Frequently Asked Questions:

Q:  Do I need a permit for all types of signs in Miami?

A: Most exterior signs require a permit in Miami-Dade County. This includes illuminated signs, storefront signs, monument signs, and even temporary banners.

Q: How long does it take to get a sign permit in Miami?

A:  The permit approval timeline varies depending on the complexity of your project and the current workload of the permitting authorities.  It can typically take several weeks to a few months.

Q: What are the common reasons for sign permit denials in Miami?

A:  Common reasons include exceeding size restrictions, improper placement, inadequate illumination, or using prohibited materials.

Q: What if my sign project requires a variance?

A:  Machin Signs can assist you with the variance application process, providing supporting documentation and justification for your request.
