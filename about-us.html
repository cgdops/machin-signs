About Us

In 1947, in Cienfuegos, Cuba, Jose Machin Sr, established Luminicos Machin. For the next two decades this family operated the business, designed, created, and maintained neon and plastic signs for major corporations across the Island, in Colombia and Venezuela.

Cafe Pilon, Texaco, Shell, Cadillac, GE, Nestle, Pepsi Cola, and many other companies made up the corporate client roster.

Forced to close its doors because of the revolution, the Machin family was forced to disperse and after many years re-established itself and Luminicos Machin under the new name Machin Signs, Inc in Miami, Florida.

Jose Machin Jr Runs and operates Machin Signs, Inc, with the same pride and professionalism that made Luminicos Machin the great success it was in Cuba and South America for many years.

Machin Signs is a fully licensed, minority-owned business, we guarantee customer satisfaction with each design and installation.
