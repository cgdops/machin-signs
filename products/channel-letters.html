Channel Letter Signs

If you’re looking to increase the visibility of your business and improve the experience of your customers, the best way to do so is with custom channel letter signs by Machin Signs. The team at Machin Signs offers a variety of building signs that can be customized to fit your needs.

General questions about channel letter signs:

What are channel letters?

Channel letters are three-dimensional individual letters that are commonly used in exterior signage on buildings. They can be illuminated or non-illuminated.

What are the benefits of channel letters?

  • High visibility: They are eye-catching and can be seen from a distance, especially when illuminated.
  • Customization: Highly customizable in terms of font, size, color, and lighting to match your branding.
  • Durability: Made from durable materials like aluminum or acrylic, they can withstand the elements.
  • Versatility: Can be mounted directly to a building facade or on a raceway for a more prominent display.
  • Modern & Professional Look: Convey a polished and sophisticated image for your business.

How much do channel letters cost?

The cost varies based on size, font, materials (aluminum vs. acrylic), illumination (LED vs. neon), and complexity of installation. Machin Signs provides free quotes tailored to your project.

How are channel letters made?

They are fabricated using materials like aluminum or acrylic. The letter shapes are cut, the returns are created, and then the faces are attached. For illuminated letters, LED modules are installed inside.

What types of channel letters are there?

  • Front-lit: The face of the letters is illuminated.
  • Back-lit/Halo-lit: The letters are illuminated from behind, creating a halo effect.
  • Open-face: No face, exposing the illumination source for a vintage look.
  • Reverse channel letters: The letters are cut out from a solid panel, creating a negative space effect.

What are the best materials for channel letters?

Aluminum is durable and ideal for exterior use. Acrylic is lightweight and allows for a wider range of colors and finishes.

How long do channel letters last?

With proper maintenance, channel letters can last for 10 years or more, especially with durable LED illumination.

Can channel letters be any font?

Yes, channel letters can be fabricated in virtually any font, allowing for brand consistency.

What are the lighting options for channel letters?

LEDs are the most energy-efficient and long-lasting option. Neon is a classic choice but requires more maintenance.

How are channel letters installed?

They are typically mounted directly to the building facade or on a raceway, which is a metal box that houses the electrical components.

Do I need a permit for channel letters?

Yes, permits are usually required for channel letter installation in Miami. Machin Signs handles the permitting process.

How do I maintain channel letters?

Regular cleaning and occasional maintenance, such as checking the electrical components, will ensure their longevity.
