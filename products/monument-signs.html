Monument Signs Miami

If you’re looking to increase the visibility of your business and improve the experience of guests, the best way to do so is with a custom monument sign by Machin Signs. The team at Machin Signs offers a variety of building signs that can be customized to fit your needs.

General questions about monument signs:

What is a monument sign?

A monument sign is a freestanding sign that is typically low to the ground and made of durable materials like brick, stone, or concrete. They often feature a combination of materials and design elements for a high-end look.

What are the benefits of monument signs?

  • Prestige and elegance: Convey a sense of permanence and professionalism.
  • Durability: Built to last and withstand the elements.
  • Versatility: Can be customized with various materials, shapes, and lighting options.
  • Increased property value: Can enhance the overall appeal of your property.

Where are monument signs typically used?

They are commonly used for:

  • Business parks
  • Retail centers
  • Housing developments
  • Schools and universities
  • Government buildings

What are the design considerations for monument signs?

  • Landscaping: Integrate the sign with the surrounding landscape for a cohesive look.
  • Lighting: Use subtle lighting to enhance visibility at night.
  • Materials: Choose materials that complement the architecture of your building.
